![CareerWise AI Preview](public/CareerWise%20AI.png)

### 📖 Overview

Most resume tools score in isolation, missing the context of the job you're targeting. CareerWise AI checks your resume against the job posting and live market data for that role.

Upload your resume, paste a job description, and get an instant match score, ATS compatibility rating, skill gap breakdown, and inline suggestions directly annotated on your resume.

Built with Next.js, OpenAI, and a multi-layer AI pipeline designed for accuracy and honest feedback.

Your resume and job description are never stored. Your data is protected by multiple layers of security, so your privacy is never a concern.
